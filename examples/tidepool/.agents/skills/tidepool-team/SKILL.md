# Tidepool: people and communication

Follows the `workplace-mapping` method. Every fact here has a source; unknowns are under "Open questions".

## Hard rules

- A completion claim goes out only with a verified receipt (Mara, 12 March 2026, #4409; N-20260312-02). Enforced by
  `rules.toml`.
- Release notices mention the person who asked for the change (Ilse, 12 March 2026, #4407; N-20260312-01). Enforced by
  `rules.toml`.

## Chats and what each is for

| Chat | Used for | Who reads it | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| release | What went live and what is about to | Everyone | One message per change, requester mentioned |
| app-team | Day-to-day questions | Mara, Jun, us | Reply inside threads (N-20260310-01) |
| on-call | Rollout windows, incidents | Okafor and whoever is on call | Do not ask product questions here (Okafor, verbal, 9 March 2026) |
